The Ottoman Empire (c. 1299–1922) was a multi-ethnic, multi-religious empire spanning Southeast Europe, Western Asia, and North Africa. Under a centralised government, it integrated diverse populations through administrative and fiscal mechanisms such as the millet system. Its military and bureaucratic institutions enabled considerable expansion until the late 17th century. Recent research challenges one-sided decline narratives but show attempts at transformation and reform in the 18th and 19th centuries.
