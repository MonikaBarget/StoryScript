Unifying the German Currency System


By 1679, the circles of Franconia, Bavaria, and Swabia sought to restore currency stability through collaboration. The Reichsmüntzordnung of 1559 faced resistance, notably from Westphalia and Burgundy. Historian Hans-Wolfgang Bergerhausen (1993) explores this struggle in Kampf um die Reichsmünzordnung. Question: What policy did Westphalia and Burgundy adopt?
