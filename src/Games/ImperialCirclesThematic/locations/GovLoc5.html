Conflict mediation was a critical function of the Imperial Circles. Jewish complaints against the nobility (as analysed by André Griemart, 2015) and top-down interventions, such as Maria Theresa's complaint against Liège's neutrality during the War of the Austrian Succession, highlight the circles' role in resolving disputes. In the 1690s, Swabia faced conflicts between knights and princes over several complaints (gravamina) in military, civil, and feudal domains. The ius Collectationis was a frequent point of contention in this case, as seen in disputes like Reichsritterschaft Bodensee vs. Stift Buchau (1691). Question: How did the imperial circle eventually balance heritage-based power and meritocracy in conflict resolution?
