Waste Removal


Waste management is a pressing issue today but did not begin with the Industrial Revolution. It was already a serious concern in densely populated cities even when European societies were still predominantly agrarian. As (urban) populations expanded, authorities struggled to maintain cleanliness and public health. Solutions included regulations on waste removal, penalties for littering, and the development of early sanitation systems.
