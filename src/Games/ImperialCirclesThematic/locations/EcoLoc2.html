Regulating Labour


Labour mobility was a critical issue in early modern Europe and craftspeople were suprisingly far-travelled already in this period (see itineraries that we can reconstruct from sources in Stadtarchiv Mainz). Authorities often imposed restrictions to retain skilled workers or prevent overcrowding in urban centers. Regulations varied by region, balancing economic needs with social stability.
