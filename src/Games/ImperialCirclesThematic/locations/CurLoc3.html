Counterfeit Currency


Nuremberg (1615) addressed counterfeit coins with abwehrung schädtlicher Finantzerey. The 1620s saw persistent issues with debased gold and silver coins. Augsburg’s 1661 list documented coin fluctuations since 1582, highlighting systemic vulnerabilities. Question:What measures would you take to enforce compliance? How would you punish those making and circulating counterfeit coins?
