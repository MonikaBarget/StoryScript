Punishment (Reichsexekution & Reichsacht)


In 1789, the Westphalian Circle failed to intervene in Liège, prompting the Emperor to call on Burgundy. Imperial Circles also addressed disobedient officials and deserters, as seen in the 1745 Cartel between Maria Theresa and the Franconian Circle. Moser (p. 50) describes measures against resistant officials, including threats of imperial intervention. Question: How effective were Reichsexekution and Reichsacht in enforcing compliance?
